Companion - Milestone 1
=======================
Needs: .NET 8 SDK (https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download) on Windows.

Run:
  1. Open a terminal in this folder.
  2. dotnet run
  (Or open Companion.csproj in Visual Studio 2022 and press F5.)

Controls:
  - He follows your cursor, stops ~170px away, and sits after ~4s of stillness.
  - Left-drag him to move him by hand.
  - Right-click him for the menu: toggle "Follow cursor", or Quit.
